PDF Image Studio

Step 1: pull the images out of a lecture PDF and let AI label them. Step 2: open a text PDF with green placeholders and let AI put the right image into each one. Everything runs in your browser.

AI settings (used by both steps)

Labeling images in Step 1 needs a model that can read images (Claude, GPT, Gemini). DeepSeek and small Llama models may fail on images. Matching images to placeholders in Step 2 only uses text, so any model works for that. Puter may ask you to sign in the first time. OpenRouter key: openrouter.ai/keys (saved only in this browser).

AI actions for these images

The AI only suggests. Flagged images get a red border, and nothing is removed until you tap Remove (or "Remove all flagged"). Removed images can be restored from the filter menu. Images that are removed or flagged are not used in Step 2. This is the only place where the AI looks at the pictures themselves, so label them here once.

✨ AI auto-fill

The AI reads only text: the words inside, before and after each green square, and each extracted image's saved label and source-page text. It never looks at the pictures again, so it is fast and uses very few credits. To fill just one placeholder, use ✨ AI pick on its card (or inside the picker that opens when you tap it in the page preview).
